Red Dwarf

A game I made with a friend during the GMTK Game Jam of 2026.

Red Dwarf is a local 2 player PVP realtime strategy game. Control your units and collect energy from the sun before it goes supernova! You win by killing the other player's home planet. Don't fly too close to the sun or you might get hit :)

You can play it on Itch.io: https://lyr-7d1h.itch.io/red-dwarf
